# Album Delivery Mode

Album Delivery Mode lets you choose how photo and video albums are handled before forwarding.

Higher modes are better suited to large or irregular albums, but the album may be sent later. No mode guarantees complete delivery.

### How to change the mode

<div><figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (263).png" alt="" width="361"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (264).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Go to:

`Forward Task Details → Edit Task → Advanced Settings → Album Delivery Mode`

Select one mode. The setting is saved automatically.

### Available modes

* **Fast** — Sends albums as quickly as possible. Suitable for most regular albums. This is the default mode.
* **Balanced** — Balances forwarding speed with the ability to group album items together. Recommended when albums are occasionally unstable.
* **Stable** — Better suited to large albums or albums whose items arrive at different times. The album may be sent later.
* **Careful** — Prioritizes careful handling of large albums or albums that often miss items. This is the slowest mode.

If you are unsure which mode to choose, use **Fast**. You can change the mode at any time.
