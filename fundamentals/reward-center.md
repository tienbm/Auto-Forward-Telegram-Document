# 🎁 Reward Center

Collected Reward Credits can be used toward eligible purchases, subscriptions, addon activations, and future promotional programs within Auto Forward Telegram.

### How to Open Reward Center

You can access Reward Center directly from the Home screen.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (259).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Steps

1. Open Auto Forward Telegram
2. Go to the Home page
3. Open the Menu
4. Tap Reward

The Reward Center dashboard will display:

* Current Reward Credit balance
* Daily check-in rewards
* Referral rewards
* Promotional reward activities
* Reward history
* Redeem code section

### Reward Credits

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (260).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Reward Credits are bonus credits earned through participation in reward programs.

Your current balance is displayed at the top of the Reward Center.

Credits may be earned from:

* Daily check-ins
* Referral commissions
* Promotional campaigns
* Reward codes
* Special events
* Future reward activities

### Daily Check-In

Earn free Reward Credits every day simply by checking in.

#### How It Works

Visit Reward Center each day and claim your daily reward.

Consecutive check-ins unlock larger rewards.

Example reward schedule:

| Day   | Reward        |
| ----- | ------------- |
| Day 1 | +0.03 Credits |
| Day 2 | +0.04 Credits |
| Day 3 | +0.05 Credits |
| Day 4 | +0.06 Credits |
| Day 5 | +0.08 Credits |
| Day 6 | +0.10 Credits |
| Day 7 | +0.15 Credits |

After completing a reward cycle, a new cycle may begin based on the current promotion rules.

#### Daily Check-In Benefits

* Free credits every day
* Higher rewards for consistent participation
* No purchases required

### Redeem a Code

Reward codes can be redeemed for instant Reward Credits.

#### How to Redeem

1. Open Reward Center
2. Locate the Redeem a Code section
3. Enter your reward code
4. Tap Redeem

If the code is valid, credits will be added immediately to your account balance.

Reward codes may be distributed through:

* Promotions
* Events
* Partnerships
* Giveaways
* Community campaigns

### Refer Friends

Earn Reward Credits by inviting new customers to Auto Forward Telegram.

#### How It Works

1. Share your referral link
2. Your friend purchases a subscription
3. You receive referral rewards

#### Referral Reward Rates

| Subscription Type | Reward |
| ----------------- | ------ |
| 1 Month Plan      | 50%    |
| 2+ Month Plans    | 30%    |
| Renewal Orders    | 5%     |

#### Additional Information

* Maximum reward per purchase: 20 Credits
* Withdrawals available from 50 Credits
* Referral rewards are credited after successful qualifying purchases

Referral percentages and conditions may change during future promotions.

### Watch Ads

Earn additional Reward Credits by watching sponsored advertisements.

#### How It Works

1. Open Reward Center
2. Navigate to Watch Ads
3. Tap Watch
4. Complete the advertisement

Credits are automatically added after successful completion.

#### Limits

Current limits may include:

* Up to 3 ads per day
* Fixed reward amount per completed ad

Daily limits may vary during promotional periods.

### Reward History

View a complete record of your reward activity.

History may include:

* Daily check-ins
* Referral commissions
* Redeem code rewards
* Advertisement rewards
* Promotional bonuses
* Reward deductions

This allows you to track how credits were earned and used.

### Reward Lounge

Reward Lounge contains special reward opportunities and promotional activities.

Available content may change over time and can include:

* Limited-time campaigns
* Seasonal promotions

* Bonus reward challenges
* Partner offers

Check regularly for new earning opportunities.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Do Reward Credits expire?

Expiration policies may vary depending on the reward source and promotional campaign.

Please refer to the specific promotion details when applicable.

#### Can I transfer Reward Credits to another user?

Reward Credits are generally tied to the account that earned them and cannot be transferred unless explicitly stated otherwise.

#### Can I withdraw Reward Credits?

Certain reward programs may support withdrawals once minimum requirements are met.

Current referral rewards support withdrawal starting from 50 Credits.

#### Why can’t I claim today’s reward?

Possible reasons include:

* Today’s reward has already been claimed
* Daily reset has not occurred yet
* Temporary network issue

Please try again later if necessary.
